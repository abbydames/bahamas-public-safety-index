# Initial launch dataset

As of October 3, 2026, the prototype includes **75 named, source-backed public records**:

- **39 conviction / guilty-plea records**
- **30 charge / arraignment records** where no conviction is asserted in the current entry
- **2 acquittal records** retained specifically so earlier accusations are not presented without their later not-guilty outcomes
- **4 appeal / status-change records** where an earlier sexual-offence conviction was quashed, altered, or otherwise requires the appellate outcome to be shown prominently

The named directory is based on publicly accessible Bahamian court, appellate, police, established-news and other reliable archival reporting. Community submissions are separate and are not promoted into the named directory merely because a user submitted an accusation.

The first research pass was weighted toward modern digital news and therefore materially undercounted historical cases. A second pass reaches back into the **1980s and 1990s**, including records from 1984, 1988, 1991, 1994, 1995, 1996 and offences committed in 1999. Historical court archives remain incomplete and difficult to search, so 75 should not be interpreted as an exhaustive total.

For non-conviction records, the interface uses the exact procedural label, such as **PUBLICLY REPORTED CHARGE — NOT A CONVICTION** or **ACQUITTED / NOT GUILTY**. If a later acquittal, dismissal, withdrawal, conviction, appeal, retrial, quashing, or other disposition is located, the record should be updated rather than leaving the earlier procedural status standing alone.

Identification photos are added only when they can be tied to the named person through a supplied public-news image, a source-specific filename or caption, or another sufficiently clear public-source connection. Generic court graphics, ambiguous thumbnails and unverified lookalikes are excluded. Photo provenance is displayed with the record when available.

Primary sources used in these research passes include the Court of Appeal of The Bahamas, the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council, BAILII, The Tribune, The Nassau Guardian, Bahamas Press, Our News, Royal Bahamas Police Force material, Bahamas Local/Nassau Guardian archives, Bahamas Court News, Amnesty International, and official Judiciary of The Bahamas judgments. Each record stores the source URL, source date where available, exact reported offence/status, and a source-check date.

This is **not an exhaustive list of sexual-offence cases in The Bahamas** and is not the Government of The Bahamas Sex Offender Register. Historical inclusion does not establish that a person is currently incarcerated, currently resident at the listed historical location, currently registered on the official government registry, or guilty when the record is labeled as a charge/allegation.

A located acquittal, quashed conviction, dismissal, withdrawal, or appeal outcome must be displayed prominently. Where sentencing or later disposition could not be located, the record says so rather than inferring an outcome.
