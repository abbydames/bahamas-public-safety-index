NAMED RECORD STANDARD

Identity needs a source

  • Name and aliases where the source identifies the person.
  • Exact status: convicted, guilty plea, charged, acquitted, appeal/retrial, or other reported posture.
  • Offence, court, case reference, conviction/sentence details when published.
  • Island and settlement/city when supported by the source.
  • Public-source photos only when rights and identity are sufficiently clear.
  • Every named profile links back to its source.

COMMUNITY REPORT STANDARD

Immediate but non-identifying

  • Firsthand or secondhand status displayed prominently.
  • Island, broad location, relationship, alleged conduct, timeframe and pattern.
  • No accused name/photo without a qualifying public source.
  • No victim name, exact address, phone number, ID, intimate image or other doxxing information.
  • No calls for retaliation or vigilantism.
  • Clearly labeled unverified and not a finding of guilt.

ACCURACY MATTERS

Request a correction or removal review

If a named record is wrong, outdated, belongs to the wrong person, has been acquitted, overturned, expunged or otherwise legally restricted, send a correction request with supporting documentation.

LEGAL + SAFETY NOTICE

Read before using this site

This project is an independent public-information and community-safety project. It is not a law-enforcement agency, court, government registry, background-check service or substitute for official records.

The Bahamas Sexual Offences Act provides for a government Sex Offender Register and Registry. Public notification is governed separately by law and ministerial regulation. This site does not claim access to the government register and does not reproduce non-public registry data.

Bahamian data-protection law treats criminal convictions and the alleged commission of an offence as sensitive personal data. A disclaimer does not make unlawful publication lawful. Named publication therefore uses source-backed records and a correction process.

Community reports: These are unverified safety signals, not findings of guilt. Do not use them to identify, accuse, threaten, stalk, harass or confront a person.

Emergency: If someone is in immediate danger or a child is being abused, contact the Royal Bahamas Police Force or the appropriate Bahamian child-protection authority rather than relying on this website.

Legal references: Sexual Offences Act, Ch. 99, ss. 26A–26J; Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act, 2014; Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act, 2014 (Appointed Day) Notice, 2019; Data Protection (Privacy of Personal Information) Act, Ch. 324A. Obtain Bahamian legal advice before launch and before enabling advertising.
