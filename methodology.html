METHODOLOGY

How information is classified

The site separates court/news-backed identity information from community-submitted safety signals so users can tell exactly what each entry means.

VERIFIED CONVICTION

Conviction / guilty plea

A named person may be listed with this label only when a qualifying source supports that the person was convicted or entered a guilty plea. Court, sentence, date and offence details should be copied only to the extent supported by the source.

PUBLICLY REPORTED CASE

Charge / allegation / appeal

A named person may be included only when a qualifying public source identifies that person. The site preserves the exact status. A charge is not a conviction. An allegation is not a finding of guilt. Acquittals, overturned convictions and appeals must be reflected.

COMMUNITY REPORT

Unverified safety signal

This is not a court finding or government record. The report appears immediately as a structured safety signal, while the accused person’s submitted identity remains encrypted and is used only for secure matching and later connection to qualifying public records.

Repeat-report matching

When a community report is submitted, the application creates a keyed cryptographic match value from the privately submitted identity. The public does not receive that identity or the secret used to create the match.

If multiple submissions match the same private identity, the community feed can show that multiple reports appear to concern the same privately identified person and display a pseudonymous cluster code. The cluster code is for connecting reports on this site, not for identifying a person in the real world.

Qualifying public sources

  1. Bahamian court or government publication.
  2. Royal Bahamas Police Force or another official authority.
  3. Established Bahamian news organization reporting identifiable court or police information.
  4. Another established news organization with a directly checkable underlying source.

Social-media posts, anonymous messages, screenshots without provenance and community reports do not, by themselves, qualify a person for a named public-record profile.

Locations

The project is Bahamas-wide. Reports can be organized by island and settlement/city, including New Providence, Grand Bahama, Abaco, Andros, Eleuthera, Exuma, Long Island, Bimini, the Berry Islands, Cat Island, San Salvador, Acklins, Crooked Island, Mayaguana, Inagua and Ragged Island.

Private street addresses are not intended for the public directory.

Photos

A photo may appear on a named, source-backed profile only where the identity is sufficiently clear and publication rights/relevance have been considered. Community reports do not publicly display a submitted photo of an accused person.

Corrections and changing case status

Named records should be revisited when a conviction is overturned, a person is acquitted, a charge is dismissed, a sentence changes, a record is legally restricted, or credible evidence shows that the wrong person was identified. Every named profile should retain its source and last-verification date.

USE RESPONSIBLY

No vigilantism

The site is intended to help people find and understand safety information. It is not a call to threaten, harass, stalk, dox, punish or physically confront anyone. Immediate danger or suspected ongoing child abuse should be reported to the appropriate Bahamian authorities.
