PRIVACY

Privacy & data handling

The project is designed so an unverified community report can warn the public without publishing the accused person’s submitted identity.

What is public

For community reports, the public API and public pages may show structured fields such as island, settlement/city, firsthand or secondhand status, relationship category, alleged conduct category, approximate time period, victim age group, pattern, date submitted, a pseudonymous private-match code, and the number of reports linked to the same privately submitted identity.

For named public-record profiles, the site may show identity and case information only where a qualifying public source supports publication and the profile clearly states the source-backed procedural status.

What is private

Community reporters may submit the accused person’s name or identifying name, aliases, approximate age/birth-year hint, a private distinguishing note, and an optional private source/evidence URL. These fields are encrypted before storage and are not returned by the public community-report API.

The system creates a keyed, non-reversible matching value from submitted identity information so repeat reports can be linked without displaying the underlying name.

Victim information

Do not submit a victim’s name, exact private address, phone number, government identification number, intimate image, medical information, school information that would identify a child, or other unnecessary identifying details. Public community reports use structured categories rather than free-form narratives to reduce accidental identification.

Security

Private identity information is designed to use AES-256-GCM encryption at rest in the application layer. Matching uses a server-side HMAC secret. Encryption keys and matching secrets must be stored as Cloudflare secrets and must never be included in client-side code, analytics, logs or GitHub.

No security system is perfect. The project should not be promoted publicly until Cloudflare Turnstile, rate limiting, access controls, backups and incident-response procedures are configured.

Accuracy, access and correction

The homepage includes a correction process for named public records. Bahamian data-protection law provides rights relating to inaccurate personal data and places duties on data controllers. Before public launch, the operator should establish a monitored process for correction, erasure and legal requests.

Retention

A final retention schedule for encrypted private identity information must be adopted before public launch following Bahamian legal advice. The application should not retain sensitive data longer than is necessary for its stated safety and matching purposes.

Advertising

Community-report pages are designed to run without AdSense by default. Advertising may be used on lower-risk pages only after policy review and approval. Advertising systems must not be given the private identity fields submitted in community reports.
