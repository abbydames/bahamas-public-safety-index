SAFETY RESOURCES

Get help now

A website report is not a substitute for emergency help, medical care, police reporting, child protection or survivor support.

Immediate danger: Call the Royal Bahamas Police Force at 911 or 919.
EMERGENCY

Royal Bahamas Police Force

911 or 919

For life-threatening emergencies, a crime in progress, assault, immediate threat of harm, or when urgent police/fire/medical response is needed.

CHILD SAFETY

Child Abuse Hotline

(242) 322-2763

The RBPF emergency-numbers page lists this Child Abuse Hotline along with Social Services at (242) 326-0526.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or 919 rather than waiting for a website response.

Anonymous crime information

RBPF crime advisories direct people with information to Crime Stoppers. The police emergency-numbers page lists (242) 328-8477, and RBPF crime-report pages also list 1-242-300-8477 for Family Islands.

Reporting sexual assault to police

The RBPF online crime-reporting guidance says that sexual-assault reports receive detective follow-up. If the situation is urgent, call 911 or 919 rather than relying on online reporting.

RBPF report-a-crime information

IMPORTANT

Protect the victim’s identity

Do not post a child’s or survivor’s name, intimate images, exact private address, medical information, school details that identify a child, or other information that could expose them to retaliation or further harm.

Verify contact information with the linked organizations if circumstances change.
